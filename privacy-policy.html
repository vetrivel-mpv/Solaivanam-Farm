Overview: SOLAIVANAM is a full-stack Farm-to-Home organic ordering and operations management application with 58mm Bluetooth ESC/POS thermal printing capabilities. We are firmly committed to user privacy and local-first data protection.

1. Information We Collect

To enable direct-from-farm grocery deliveries, we collect the following user-provided data:

  • Contact Details: Customer Name and Phone Number (used to coordinate deliveries and generate invoice summaries).
  • Delivery Location: Apartment community name, block, unit number, and delivery notes.
  • Order History: Quantities of fresh produce (Keerai varieties, cold-pressed oils, grains, and herbal podis), pricing totals, and order timestamps.

2. Hardware & Device Permissions (Bluetooth)

Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN with neverForLocation):
These permissions are exclusively required to scan for and connect to paired 58mm ESC/POS Bluetooth thermal printers (such as the Niyama BT-58) to print delivery receipts, Tamil language typography rasterized slips, and farm harvest batches.

No Location Tracking: Our app does NOT monitor, record, or transmit GPS or physical location data.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Local-First Database: All data is persisted locally on the device using an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database via Android Jetpack Room.
  • Zero Commercial Monetization: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third-party advertising companies.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users have full autonomy over their personal data:

  • In-App Purge: You can wipe all local orders and customer details directly using the in-app "Clear Local Data" button found within the Privacy Policy modal.
  • Manual Requests: To request complete deletion or clarification regarding your data, email us directly at vetrivelmpv02@gmail.com.
